# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Business owners and sales teams managing quotes and budgets for Argentine businesses. Primary users are business owners handling the full quote lifecycle (draft → sent → paid). Secondary users have limited actions. Context: daily office/admin work, data-heavy workflows around quotes, clients, payments, and statistics.

## Product Purpose

Quote & Budget Management System for Argentine businesses. Manages the full quote lifecycle, client balances, custom pricing, payments, and statistics. Success means business owners can efficiently create quotes, track payments, and manage client relationships without friction.

## Brand Personality

Professional, efficient, trustworthy. The design gets out of the way and lets users focus on their work. No unnecessary flair—clarity and speed matter more than visual spectacle.

## Anti-references

- Consumer-app playfulness (serious business tool, not social)
- Dark mode as default (office environments with varied lighting)
- Excessive motion/animations (slows down daily workflows)
- Marketing-site patterns (hero sections, flashy reveals, scroll-driven storytelling)
- SaaS-dashboard clichés (gradient text, glassmorphism, identical card grids)

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration**: Data tables, forms, and status indicators must be immediately scannable. No visual noise competing with content.
2. **Efficiency first**: Minimize clicks, maximize information density without crowding. Users do this daily—respect their time.
3. **Trustworthy consistency**: Predictable patterns, clear feedback, no surprises. Business owners need to trust the tool.
4. **Argentine locale awareness**: Number formatting (thousands separator `.`, decimal `,`), Spanish UI, cultural context for pricing and business workflows.
5. **Progressive disclosure**: Show what's needed now, hide complexity until requested. Don't overwhelm with every possible action at once.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High contrast for data-heavy tables and forms. Proper focus states for keyboard navigation. Reduced motion support for any transitions. Color-blind-safe status indicators (not relying on color alone for quote states).
